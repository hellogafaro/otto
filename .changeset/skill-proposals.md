Bots can propose a new skill or a fix to one with skill_create and skill_update. A person approves it in the chat and becomes the skill's creator: the bot's owner for a skill only that bot uses, a workspace member who can make changes for a Team skill, or someone who can edit the skill for a fix. A fix is refused if the skill changed since the bot read it, and built-in skills can't be changed. The skill's page shows which bot proposed it, in which chat, and who approved it.
