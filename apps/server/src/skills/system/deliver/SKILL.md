---
name: deliver
description: Use when work is finished and checked, to post the result where it was asked with its proof, update the task and name the next step.
---

- Keep failed and unverified checks visible.
- Name the exact state: draft, PR open, merged, deployed, checked live.
- Move what people need out of your computer: approved files to the Library, code to a PR. Drafts go in the chat as attachments until a person approves one.
- Chat: message_post with the result first and the proof attached.
- Task: task_comment_create the result, links and proof. Leave it in progress and ask a person to review, unless they asked you to complete it.
- Name the next step and who owns it.
- When the same kind of request has come up two or three times, propose a routine (routine_create) or a skill (skill_create).
- Not for unfinished work another owner continues (handoff) or a recap that changes nothing (summarize).
