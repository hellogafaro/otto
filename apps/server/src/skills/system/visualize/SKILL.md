name: visualize
description: Use when seeing beats reading, for screenshots as proof, before and after comparisons, design options, diagrams, charts or an HTML page.
---

- Say whether a visual is observed evidence, a proposal or an illustration. A mockup proves nothing exists.
- A proof screenshot shows a check verify chose; it is not a passed check on its own. Back it with the record behind it (API result, saved row, log).
- Before and after: capture before you change anything, then after under the same conditions. Without a real baseline, say so.
- Options: two or three genuinely different ones, with your pick and why.
- Diagrams: a mermaid block. Charts: plot real values with a script and check the image. Never invent data.
- Pages: one self-contained HTML file, see [page](references/page.md).
- Look at what you made before sending. Attach images with message_post or task_comment files.
