name: visualize
description: Use when seeing beats reading, for designs, mockups, prototypes, plans and PRDs, dense data, diagrams, charts, or screenshots as proof and before and after.
---

- Pick the format by what it is. Designs, mockups, prototypes, plans, PRDs, proposals and dense data: an HTML page, see [page](references/page.md). Proof that something exists (a bug, a live screen, before and after): a screenshot. A quick flow inside a chat reply: a mermaid block.
- Otto renders HTML natively, so the page is the deliverable. Never send a screenshot of a page you built in its place.
- Make it interactive where that helps people understand or decide: rows expand, tabs switch between options, states toggle, filters narrow data, live states tick. Skip interaction that is only decoration.
- Match fidelity to the question. Flow or structure: a clean wireframe. Look and feel: high fidelity, with the product's real colors, type, spacing and copy.
- Plans and PRDs: one page that mixes text, diagrams (flows, sequences, timelines), tables and mockups of key screens, with a contents bar when it is long. Draft the words with write first.
- Say whether a visual is observed evidence, a proposal or an illustration. A mockup proves nothing exists.
- A proof screenshot shows a check verify chose; it is not a passed check on its own. Back it with the record behind it (API result, saved row, log).
- Before and after: capture before you change anything, then after under the same conditions. Without a real baseline, say so.
- Options: two or three genuinely different ones in one page, with your pick and why.
- Charts: plot real values. Never invent data; label sample data as sample.
- Look at what you made before sending: open it, screenshot it at desktop and mobile widths, click every interaction and check every diagram rendered. Those screenshots are for you, not the deliverable.
- Drafts stay on your computer. Attach each version with message_post files (name-v1.html, name-v2.html). Save to the Library only the version a person approves, and say it is final.
