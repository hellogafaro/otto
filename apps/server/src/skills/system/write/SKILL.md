---
name: write
description: Use when drafting or editing a long or important text, such as a report, proposal or announcement; not for chat replies or short notes.
---

- Lead with the answer. Keep only what the reader needs to understand it and act.
- Keep facts exact: code, ids, numbers, dates, errors, links. Keep risks and the next step.
- Match the reader's language and tone. Plain words, active voice, sentence case.
- No filler, hype, chatbot phrases, em dashes or bold-label lists.
- For a long or important text, check [patterns](references/patterns.md).
- A plan, PRD or proposal people will review becomes a page: draft the words here, then build it with visualize.
- A document or other long text is heavy work: once drafted, run verify, then review, then deliver.
