# Patterns to cut

- Puffery and promotion: pivotal, testament to, vibrant, groundbreaking, stunning. Say what happened.
- Vague attribution: "experts say". Name the source or cut it.
- AI vocabulary: additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, foster, intricate, landscape, showcase, underscore, leverage, utilize.
- "Serves as", "boasts": say "is" or "has".
- "Not just X, but Y"; groups of three by habit; synonym cycling; "from X to Y" with no scale.
- Abstract metaphors: substrate, vector, primitive, north star, flywheel, paradigm. Use the concrete word.
- Em dashes, colons as connectors, bold everywhere, title case, decorative emojis, curly quotes.
- Chatbot lines: "Great question", "Certainly", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if".
- Filler: "in order to", "due to the fact that", "it is important to note", stacked hedges, generic endings.
- Feelings instead of mechanisms. Name the mechanism or the number.
- Over-compression: keep articles and verbs, spell out arrows and abbreviations.
